Questions

Everything you need to know.

Do I need a gym to work with you?

No. Programs are fully built around your situation gym, home, hotel, minimal equipment. We design around your real life, not the other way around.

How long until I see results?

Most clients see clear physical and mindset changes within the first 4 weeks. The full transformation typically lands in the 12–16 week window.

I'm a beginner is this for me?

Absolutely. The majority of my clients start as beginners. You don't need to be fit to start you need to start to get fit.

Is the nutrition plan restrictive?

Never. No banned foods, no extremes. We build flexible nutrition around your preferences, your culture and your schedule.

How do we communicate?

Through WhatsApp and a private coaching app, plus video check-ins. You're never coaching alone.
