My Story — From Passion to Purpose

My journey with movement began when I was just eight years old, growing up in Brazil. Dancing was my first passion, and it taught me the importance of discipline, dedication, self-expression, and consistency from a young age.

But my journey into fitness wasn’t always straightforward.

In 2011, I moved to Australia, leaving behind everything familiar to start a new chapter. Like many people, I faced challenges adapting to a new country, finding my direction, and building a routine.

During that time, my own health and fitness weren’t where I wanted them to be. I lacked structure, struggled with consistency, and often felt lost when it came to training.

That changed when I decided to invest in myself and work with a personal trainer.

What started as a physical transformation became something much bigger. I discovered the power of structured training, better habits, discipline, and having someone guide and believe in my potential.

I didn’t just transform my body. I transformed my mindset, my confidence, and the direction of my life.

The journey

From Dancing to Bodybuilding and Powerlifting

As my passion for fitness grew, I challenged myself in new ways.

I stepped into the world of bodybuilding, preparing for and competing in a bodybuilding show. That experience taught me about dedication, nutrition, body composition, and the mental strength required to pursue a challenging goal.

My journey then evolved into powerlifting, where I developed a deeper appreciation for strength, performance, technique, and progressive overload.

From the creativity and discipline of dance to the physical demands of bodybuilding and the strength of powerlifting, every chapter helped shape the coach I am today.

But the most valuable lesson I learned was that real transformation is about so much more than appearance.

It’s about what you become through the process.

My purpose

Turning My Passion Into My Purpose

Inspired by my own transformation, I pursued further education in health and fitness, including a Diploma and Bachelor of Health Science, and built my career as a fitness professional.

Over the past six years, I’ve developed my personal training business in Australia, helping clients transform their physiques, improve their strength, build confidence, and create healthier lifestyles.

I’ve had the privilege of working with people of different ages, abilities, and backgrounds, each with their own challenges and goals.

And while seeing physical results is incredibly rewarding, what inspires me most is watching someone become more confident, capable, and proud of themselves.

How I coach

My Coaching Philosophy

I believe lasting results come from the combination of strength training, nutrition, mindset, and sustainable habits.

My approach isn’t about quick fixes, extreme restrictions, or chasing perfection.

It’s about building a realistic structure that works for your lifestyle, understanding your body, and developing the consistency to keep progressing long after the initial motivation fades.

I know what it feels like to start from scratch, to feel uncertain, and to need guidance. I’ve experienced firsthand how powerful the right support and structure can be.

That’s why my mission is to help you discover what you’re truly capable of, physically and mentally.

Because fitness isn’t just about changing how you look. It’s about changing how you feel, how you live, and what you believe is possible for yourself.
